Why MedIndex

Everything you need to choose, in one place

No more opening four clinic websites in four tabs. MedIndex puts the facts that matter next to each other.

  • Real prices, with sources

    Consultation prices come from each network's public price page, with a link to the source and the date it was collected.

  • Distance from you

    Share your location or pick an area of Bucharest and sort clinics by how far away they are.

  • CNAS coverage

    See which services the clinic says can be covered by Romania's state health insurance, usually with a referral.

  • Seniority at a glance

    Filter or sort by medical rank, from specialist physician to senior consultant and primary physician.

  • AI assistant with 112 safety

    Describe your symptoms and get a suggested type of specialist. Emergency signs are caught first and you are told to call 112.

  • Patient reviews

    Rate doctors you have seen and read what other patients wrote, so the next person can choose with more confidence.

How it works

From "something's wrong" to a booked visit in three steps

MedIndex helps you choose. Booking always happens directly with the clinic.

  1. 1

    Describe or search

    Search a specialty or a doctor's name, or tell MedIndex Assistant what is bothering you.

  2. 2

    Compare the facts

    Filter by network and CNAS, then sort by price, distance or seniority to find your best fit.

  3. 3

    Book with the clinic

    Open the doctor's official profile, confirm the current price and book directly with the clinic.
